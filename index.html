Tinnitus Multi-Therapy

Three sound therapies for tinnitus, each with its own volume. Run one, or run them together.

Nature sounds

Ten background loops: crickets, cicadas, wind, two kinds of moving water, ocean, a crackling fire, a forest, a coffee shop. Each one loops without a join you can hear. The brightest sit at the top of the list, since those are the ones that reach a high-pitched tinnitus.

ACRN

Acoustic Coordinated Reset. Four quiet tones sit close around your tinnitus pitch, two below and two above. They play in random order, with a short gap after each. In tinnitus the nerves tuned to nearby pitches tend to fire in lockstep, and random order is meant to break that up.

Spectral Ripple

A shimmering wash built from many tones at once. Their volumes rise and fall in a pattern that never settles, so no two pitches near your tinnitus hold a steady relationship for long. It sounds a bit like moving air.

Pitch matching

Hold the frequency slider. A tone sounds and follows your thumb as you drag, then stops when you let go. Switch it between a clear tone and a hiss, whichever is closer to your own tinnitus.

Playback pauses while you are setting the pitch, and picks up afterwards.

Privacy

The app contains no networking code, so it cannot send anything anywhere even if it wanted to. There is no account to create. Your settings live on your phone.

Research

ACRN follows Tass and colleagues (2012). Spectral Ripple follows Yukhnovich and colleagues (2025).

This app implements the methods those papers describe. It is not the equipment either study used, and nobody has tested whether it produces the same results.

Start at low volume and only turn it up if it feels okay. Stop if your tinnitus gets worse or anything hurts. Talk to a doctor or audiologist for medical advice about your tinnitus.
