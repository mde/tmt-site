Tinnitus Multi-Therapy

Three tinnitus sound therapies, each with its own volume, so you can run one, or blend all three.

Nature sounds

Ten background loops: crickets, cicadas, wind, a river, a stream, ocean surf, a crackling fire, a forest, a coffee shop.

ACRN

Acoustic Coordinated Reset Neuromodulation. Four quiet tones sit close around your tinnitus pitch, two below and two above, and play in a shuffled order with a short gap after each. In tinnitus the nerves tuned to nearby pitches tend to fire together; the random order is meant to break that up.

Spectral Ripple

A shimmering wash built from many tones at once, whose volumes rise and fall in a pattern that never settles, so no two pitches near your tinnitus hold a steady relationship.

Pitch matching your tinnitus

Hold the frequency slider and a tone follows the slider, stopping when you let go. Switch between a clear tone and a hiss, whichever is closer to your own tinnitus. This pitch determines the pitch of the therapy modes.

Built to leave running

Can play in the background. Mixes with music and podcasts instead of interrupting them.

Privacy

The app contains no networking code, so it cannot send anything anywhere. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your settings stay on your phone.

Free and paid

The free version plays one therapy at a time. Unlock simultaneous playback with a one-time purchase. No subscription.

Research

ACRN follows Tass and colleagues (2012). Spectral Ripple follows Yukhnovich and colleagues (2025).

This app implements the methods those papers describe. It is not the equipment the studies used, and there is no guarantee it produces the same results.

Start at low volume and only turn it up if it feels okay. Stop if your tinnitus gets worse or anything hurts. Talk to a doctor or audiologist for medical advice about your tinnitus.
