Privacy Policy

Tinnitus Multi-Therapy. Last updated 2 September 2026.

This app collects no data. Not anonymized, not aggregated, not diagnostic. No account, no analytics, no identifiers. There is nothing to opt out of.

Network access

The app contains no networking code. It never connects to a server, so it cannot transmit anything about you or how you use it. It behaves identically in airplane mode.

Tracking and third parties

No analytics, no advertising, no third-party crash reporting, no third-party frameworks. You cannot create an account because there are none.

Stored data

The app saves your settings on your device, using the system's standard preferences store: tinnitus frequency, volumes, hearing profile, which therapies are enabled, and which nature sound you picked. They stay there. Your own encrypted device backups will include them if you make backups, and deleting the app deletes them.

Permissions

The app plays audio with the screen off, which iOS allows it to declare without asking you. It requests no permissions at all, and does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or health data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

Any revision appears here with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, a change could only ever describe the same, or less.

Contact
